# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two groups with distinct contexts:

**Ouders en donateurs** — Emotionally connected to the school. They visit the donation page from a phone link or class newsletter. They need to quickly understand what the donation goal is, feel moved to contribute, and act without friction. Trust is essential; they should never feel like they're on a commercial sales page.

**School- of klasbeheerder** — A teacher or coordinator who monitors the campaign progress and manages tasks via the dashboard. Works at a desk, needs a calm and clear overview. Functional efficiency matters more than visual excitement.

## Product Purpose

A school donation campaign platform for OBS De Springplank (or similar primary schools). The platform enables parents to donate toward a class goal, and gives school staff a simple dashboard to monitor progress. The site should feel like a natural extension of the school community — not a startup product, not a government portal.

## Brand Personality

Warm, betrouwbaar, menselijk.

The interface is welcoming to all parents regardless of tech confidence. It speaks in plain language, shows real impact, and avoids anything that feels commercial or distant. The donation page may be emotionally engaging; the dashboard must be calm and focused.

## Anti-references

- **Gemeente-portaal / overheidswebsite** — cold, bureaucratic, form-heavy. No.
- **Speelse schoolposter** — bubble fonts, crayon colors, kindergarten energy. No.
- **Generiek SaaS-blauw** — neon gradients, hero-metric cards, glass-morphic panels. No.
- **Zware dark-mode admin** — terminal-like dashboards, hacker aesthetic. No.
- **Commerciële landingspagina** — countdown timers, aggressive CTAs, fake social proof. No.

The right lane: light, warm, trustworthy, concrete, and human.

## Design Principles

1. **Concreet boven abstract** — Show real amounts, real names, real goals. Not "join our mission" but "€840 van €1.200 opgehaald".
2. **Vertrouwen door rust** — Whitespace, clear hierarchy, and calm colors build trust faster than effects and animations.
3. **Menselijke taal** — Every label, button, and error message should sound like a person, not a system.
4. **Donatiepagina = emotie, dashboard = focus** — The two surfaces have different emotional registers. The donation page earns attention; the dashboard rewards it.
5. **Mobile-first realisme** — Parents tap on phones in hallways. Every touch target, form input, and CTA must work at 375px without zooming or squinting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum for all text/background combinations.
- Touch targets minimum 44×44px.
- Focus states visible and keyboard-navigable throughout.
- Reduced motion: respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all CSS animations.
- Color is never the only signal for meaning (errors, states, progress).
